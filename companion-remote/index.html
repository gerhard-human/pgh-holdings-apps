Companion Remote

A remote-control app for compatible Apple TV devices on your local network. Use buttons or swipe navigation, your phone's keyboard, app shortcuts and optional search of your own Plex library or a catalogue service.

Companion Remote is an independent app by PGH Holdings (Pty) Ltd. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Apple Inc. Apple TV is a trademark of Apple Inc.

Before you use it

You need an Android device running Android 8 or later and a compatible Apple TV on the same local network. Pairing needs the code shown by your Apple TV. Available volume, playback and media details depend on your TV, receiver and current app. The phone cannot send the physical Apple remote's infrared volume signal.

Support

For help, email pgh-holdings-apps@proton.me. Support is in English by email only. Please describe your Android and Apple TV models, software versions and the steps that led to the problem. Do not send a pairing code, API key, token or pairing record.

If Do Not Disturb hides Now Playing, allow Companion Remote in your phone's Do Not Disturb app notifications.
