Companion Remote

An Android remote for Apple TV. Published by PGH Holdings (Pty) Ltd.

Companion Remote controls a compatible Apple TV from an Android phone over your home network: a direction pad and swipe navigation, playback controls, an app launcher, your phone's keyboard for typing on the television, and Now Playing with a notification while you are out of the app. It is a single upfront purchase on Google Play, with every feature included and no adverts, tracking, subscription or account.

What you need

  • An Android phone running Android 8 or later.
  • A compatible Apple TV on the same local network. The app is designed for Apple TV 4K.
  • A network that lets the two devices find each other. Guest Wi-Fi, a VPN or router device isolation can prevent this.

Support
